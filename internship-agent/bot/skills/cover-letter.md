# Cover letter

1. Collect: company, role, posting text or bullets, student's relevant projects/classes, why this team.
2. If facts are missing, ask up to three targeted questions. Draft anyway with `TODO` for gaps.
3. Structure:
   - Opening: role + one specific hook from the posting
   - 1–2 short paragraphs tied to their experience
   - Close: availability term (summer/fall) and thanks
4. Keep under 350 words. No invented internships or awards.
5. After the draft, offer to `track_application` with status `drafting`.
